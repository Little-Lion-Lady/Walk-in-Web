Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Dine now

  • Dine now is free to use for a limited time, so you can try it without paying a claim fee.

  • A First Table reservation is planned in advance, and gives diners 50% off food. A Dine now offer is available within 15 minutes of dining time. Diners claim the offer, arrive within the arrival window, and get 30% off food.

  • No. The 30% comes off your food bill only. Drinks are charged as normal.

  • Arrive within the time slot you claimed to be sure of getting the discount. If you turn up after it, honouring the offer is up to the venue.

  • The venue may ask you to wait. If that doesn't suit you and you've been charged a booking fee, you'll have that refunded back to your account.

  • You can cancel your Dine now claim any time before the arrival slot starts.
